# FMCG Sales Analysis Dashboard -- Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **FMCG Sales Analysis Dashboard**
built with **Microsoft Power BI**.

The dashboard analyzes sales performance across **time, product
categories, subcategories, brands, sales channels, inventory, units
sold, and discounts**. It is designed to provide a clear business view
of revenue performance and product/channel contribution.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Business Objectives

-   Monitor overall sales and inventory KPIs.
-   Analyze monthly sales trends and identify high/low-performing
    periods.
-   Compare sales performance across product categories and brands.
-   Understand the contribution of different sales channels.
-   Identify high-performing product subcategories.
-   Compare units sold with available stock.
-   Analyze discount levels across subcategories.
-   Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

# 🔎 Key Business Insights

## 1. Monthly Sales Performance

Net sales show noticeable variation throughout the year.

-   **July** recorded the highest monthly net sales at approximately
    **42.5M**.
-   **December** followed closely at approximately **42.4M**.
-   **August** reached approximately **42.2M**.
-   **February** recorded the lowest monthly net sales at approximately
    **35.5M**.
-   September also showed a noticeable decline to approximately
    **37.3M**.

### Insight

Sales performance is strongest around the middle and end of the year,
while February and September represent weaker periods that could be
investigated for seasonality, demand changes, or promotional
differences.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Sales by Category

Net sales by category show a clear concentration in the leading
categories:

  Category          Net Sales
  --------------- -----------
  Snacks                 130M
  Beverages              104M
  Personal Care           90M
  Dairy                   84M
  Home Care               65M

### Insight

**Snacks and Beverages** are the largest contributors to net sales,
while **Home Care** has the lowest contribution among the displayed
categories.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Sales by Brand

The leading brands by net sales are:

-   **BrandD:** 93M
-   **BrandB:** 91M
-   **BrandC:** 79M
-   **BrandF:** 75M
-   **BrandA:** 70M
-   **BrandE:** 65M

### Insight

BrandD and BrandB are the two largest contributors, while BrandE records
the lowest sales among the displayed brands.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Sales by Channel

The dashboard shows a strong concentration of sales in the
**Hypermarket** channel:

-   **Hypermarket:** 259M --- approximately 55%
-   **E-commerce:** 113M --- approximately 24%
-   **Supermarket:** 89M --- approximately 19%
-   **Convenience:** 13M --- approximately 3%

### Insight

Hypermarkets are the dominant sales channel, contributing more than half
of the displayed net sales. E-commerce is the second-largest channel.

## 📦 Units Sold

The product analysis also compares the number of units sold by
subcategory.

The highest unit volumes are concentrated in products such as:

-   Soda
-   Toothpaste
-   Chocolate
-   Water
-   Chips

### Insight

High unit volume does not necessarily mean the highest sales value,
making it useful to analyze both **units sold and net sales** when
evaluating product performance.
